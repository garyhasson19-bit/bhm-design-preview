Frequently asked questions

The questions we get most often, with short answers. If yours isn’t here, write to us.

Understanding

What is GEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) makes your business easy for an AI assistant like ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI to cite when a customer asks where to go.

How is it different from local SEO?

Local SEO targets the results of Google and Google Maps. GEO targets the answers an AI writes. Both feed on the same information: if it’s accurate and consistent, you win on both.

Why does my Google listing matter for AI too?

Assistants lean on online sources: your listing, your website, directories, reviews. When those sources contradict each other, an AI has a harder time citing you. Fixing the listing is the first step for both.

Is my business a fit?

If your customers look you up online before they come (restaurant, café, hotel, plumber, hairdresser, practice, shop), yes.

Pricing and timing

How much does it cost?

The initial setup (listing, website, pages) starts from €200 per service, for one business. Because it’s a process and not a one-off setting, the follow-up is priced by the month. You get both prices after the free audit, before you commit to anything.

Why a monthly rate?

Because it can’t be done once and forgotten. Hours change, new reviews come in, AI assistants change their sources, competitors move. Monthly follow-up also keeps your account up to date (Google listing: photos, hours, details), keeps your details accurate and tracks what moved.

What’s in the free audit?

A short document with screenshots: the state of your listing, what’s missing or wrong, what Google and a few AI assistants answer when someone searches for your kind of business in your town, and what we suggest. No credit card, no obligation to continue.

How soon will I see a change?

A corrected Google listing shows quickly. Rankings and AI citations move in weeks, sometimes longer. We don’t promise a date.

Do you guarantee ChatGPT will cite me?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. AI assistants change their sources. We make your information clear and consistent: that’s what improves your chances.

Working together

What do I need to do?

Give us manager access to the Google listing (we guide you), a few recent photos, and approve the texts. Allow fifteen minutes to get started.

Do I have to change my website?

Not necessarily. Often we rework a few pages and add a frequently-asked-questions page.

Can you remove a negative review?

No. Only Google can remove a review, and only if it breaks its rules. We reply to reviews, we don’t fabricate them.

Who works on my account?

Gary and his father. You have one contact person, from the first message to the follow-up.

Which languages do you work in, and where?

English, French and Dutch. Remotely, for businesses all over the world.

What do you do with the form data?

It’s only used to answer your request. Everything is explained on the privacy page. See the privacy policy
