FRNLEN

Restaurants: be found before mealtime.

In Knokke at the height of summer, or on a Thursday night near Rue Dansaert in Brussels, the decision is made in seconds on a phone. Your listing has to answer before the customer changes their mind.

What we often see

  • A PDF menu that’s unreadable on a phone
  • A kitchen that closes at 9.30 pm, a listing that says 11 pm
  • Reviews about the service left unanswered
  • Not one photo of the dining room or terrace this year

What we do

  • Menu as text, recent photos of dishes, a booking link that works
  • Real kitchen hours, including closing days
  • Short, polite replies to reviews
  • Precise categories: “Italian restaurant”, not just “restaurant”

What people ask AI assistants

“A quiet Italian restaurant in Knokke, with a terrace, open on Sunday evening.”

If your listing and website don’t say “terrace” and “Sunday evening”, the AI can’t recommend you.
