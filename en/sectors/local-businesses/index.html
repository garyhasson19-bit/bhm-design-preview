Local businesses: be the first call.

When the boiler dies, people type “plumber” and their town, then call the first one who picks up. For these trades, the Google listing is often the only website.

What we often see

  • A service area left blank
  • Missing services: the firm does emergency repairs, the listing only says “plumbing”
  • A phone number nobody answers any more
  • Not one photo of finished work

What we do

  • Service area, services and emergency hours filled in
  • Phone number and links checked one by one
  • Photos of jobs, with your agreement
  • A review request after every job: a link, a QR code or an NFC plaque

What people ask AI assistants

“A plumber who can come tonight in Uccle.”

The AI only names what it could read: town, services, availability. If it isn’t written down, you don’t exist for it.
