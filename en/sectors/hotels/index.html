Hotels: show the room before the platform does.

Travellers compare your Google listing with a booking platform. If your listing is thin, they book elsewhere, or through a platform that takes a commission.

What we often see

  • A booking button that goes to a platform, not to the hotel’s own site
  • Room photos from before the renovation
  • No online answer about parking, breakfast, pets
  • Reviews in one language, never answered in that language

What we do

  • A direct booking link, prominent on the listing
  • Current photos of rooms and shared spaces, in a sensible order
  • A frequently-asked-questions page: parking, breakfast, pets, late arrival
  • Replies to reviews in the guest’s language

What people ask AI assistants

“A hotel in Knokke with parking, dogs welcome, a short walk from the beach.”

When the answers (parking, dogs) are written nowhere, the AI guesses, sometimes wrongly. Better that it reads yours.
