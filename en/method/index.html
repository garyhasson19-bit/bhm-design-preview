Method: four steps, one contact person.

No jargon, no forty-page report. At every step you know what we’re doing and what we need from you.

  1. 1

    Diagnosis

    What we do
    We look at your listing, your website, your reviews. We search for your type of business in your town on Google and with a few AI assistants, and note who comes up.
    What you get
    A short document with screenshots: what’s missing, what’s wrong, what’s urgent.
    What we ask of you
    The Google Maps link of your business.
  2. 2

    Action plan

    What we do
    We rank the actions by usefulness and cost. You decide what we do and what you’d rather leave for later.
    What you get
    A list of actions, an order, a price for each block.
    What we ask of you
    Fifteen minutes to talk it through, by phone or WhatsApp.
  3. 3

    Delivery

    What we do
    We do the work: listing fixed, reviews answered, details matched across your site and directories, pages reworked.
    What you get
    An up-to-date listing and website, and the list of what changed.
    What we ask of you
    Manager access to the listing (we guide you), a few recent photos, approval of the texts.
  4. 4

    Monthly follow-up

    What we do
    Every month: we check the details, handle new reviews, and record your position and your mentions in AI assistants. This follow-up has a monthly rate.
    What you get
    One page, in plain language: what moved and what we suggest next.
    What we ask of you
    Tell us when something changes: menu, hours, closure.
