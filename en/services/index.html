Three services. Each starts from a specific problem.

We don’t sell a “visibility pack”. We look at what’s wrong in your case and fix it in that order.

Google Business Profile optimisation

Your Google Business Profile is your business’s ID card in Google and Google Maps: address, hours, photos, reviews.

From €200

Initial setup, starting price for one business.

The problem

Old photos, wrong hours, vague categories, reviews with no reply. The customer hesitates, or picks someone else.

What we do
  • Full audit of the listing, with screenshots
  • Categories, description, attributes and services filled in
  • Photos sorted and added. If some are missing, we tell you what to shoot
  • Regular hours, bank holidays and one-off closures up to date
  • Replies written for reviews still waiting
  • Links to your website, booking page and menu checked

Expected result : An accurate, complete listing you’re happy to show. Customers find the right information first time, and Google has what it needs to rank you.

Local SEO

Local SEO means showing up in the results when someone searches near you: “café Ixelles”, “hotel Knokke”.

From €200

Initial setup, starting price for one business.

The problem

You exist, but for “italian restaurant Knokke” three competitors come up before you.

What we do
  • Research into the words your customers really type, town by town
  • Name, address and phone number identical everywhere: directories, social media, website
  • Pages of your website reworked for those searches: titles, text, structured data
  • An easier way to ask for reviews: a link, and an NFC plaque if you like
  • Tracking of your position on a few key searches

Expected result : More presence on the searches that matter to you, with the same details everywhere. Rankings move in weeks, not days. We tell you that up front.

GEO: getting cited by AI

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) makes your business easy for ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI to cite.

From €200

Initial setup, starting price for one business.

The problem

A customer asks ChatGPT “where can I have brunch in Brussels?”. If your details are scattered or contradict each other, the AI cites someone else.

What we do
  • We ask several AI assistants your customers’ questions and note who gets cited
  • A clear, identical description of your business: cuisine, neighbourhood, languages spoken
  • A frequently-asked-questions page, with your customers’ real questions
  • Structured data on your website, so machines read your details correctly
  • Presence on the sources AI assistants consult: directories, local press, reviews

Expected result : A better chance of being cited. Nobody can guarantee a citation: AI assistants change their sources. We say so from the start.

Then: monthly follow-up

GEO and local SEO aren’t set once and forgotten. Hours change, new reviews come in, AI assistants change their sources, competitors move. It’s a process, so the follow-up is priced by the month: we keep your account up to date (Google listing: photos, hours, details), keep your details accurate and show you what moved.

You get the exact price after the free audit, before you commit to anything. You get the monthly rate after the free audit, together with the price for the initial setup.
